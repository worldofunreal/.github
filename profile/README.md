# World of Unreal

Independent game studio. We make strategy games and the tools around them.

[Shadows of War](https://github.com/worldofunreal/shadows-of-war) is our current game: multiplayer territory strategy in Rust, free to play in the browser at [shadowsofwar.io](https://shadowsofwar.io). Source is open (AGPL-3.0).

[Cosmicrafts](https://github.com/cosmicrafts) is our Web3 strategy franchise, developed in its own org.

What we build in the open:

- [wou-id](https://github.com/worldofunreal/wou-id): identity engine for our games. Email OTP, OAuth, wallets. Rust.
- [Ionic-Swap](https://github.com/worldofunreal/Ionic-Swap): cross-chain swap interface. Astro, Rust.
- [nftropoly](https://github.com/worldofunreal/nftropoly): marketplace for game items and collectibles.
- [Signal](https://github.com/worldofunreal/Signal): daily focus app. Vue.
- [IC_Hub_Unity](https://github.com/worldofunreal/IC_Hub_Unity): Unity portal for Web3 apps.
