World of Unreal is an independent AI and Web3 studio. We build games, apps and digital art tools, plus the infrastructure they share: identity, launchers and marketplaces.

## Shadows of War

![Shadows of War](https://worldofunreal.com/assets/shadowsofwar-banner.webp)

Multiplayer territory strategy written in Rust. Pick one of twelve historical commanders, build cities and industry on a real world map, form alliances and take territory from other players in real time. Free to play in the browser, no download. The full source is open under AGPL-3.0: deterministic lockstep server, relay network and WASM client.

[Play at shadowsofwar.io](https://shadowsofwar.io) · [Source code](https://github.com/worldofunreal/shadows-of-war)

## Cosmicrafts

![Cosmicrafts](https://worldofunreal.com/assets/cosmicrafts.jpg)

Our space strategy franchise with a Web3 economy: fleet battles, ranked ladders, missions and rewards, with items and currency that players own. Alpha is live.

[cosmicrafts.com](https://cosmicrafts.com) · [github.com/cosmicrafts](https://github.com/cosmicrafts)
